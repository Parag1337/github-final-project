# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator to calculate Simple Interest.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal amount
- R = Rate of interest per year
- T = Time in years

## Example

If:

- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

## Features

- Calculates simple interest
- Easy to understand
- Uses the standard simple interest formula

## Calculation

The calculator takes three inputs:

1. Principal amount
2. Rate of interest
3. Time period

It then calculates the simple interest using:

`SI = (P × R × T) / 100`
